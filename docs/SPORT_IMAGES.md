# Sport photos

Sport cards and the court-games carousel show a real photo when one is available, in this order:

1. The **Image URL** an administrator saves on the sport (Admin > Sports). Use a durable HTTPS address.
2. A self-hosted file at `src/SportsClubHub.Web/wwwroot/images/sports/<key>.jpg`.
3. Nothing: the card keeps its coloured category panel with the sport's initial.

## File keys

football, rugby, cricket, hockey, netball, basketball, volleyball, tennis, badminton, swimming, waterpolo,
surfing, athletics, running, boxing, judo, taekwondo, chess, tabletennis, squash, cycling, archery, hiking.

## Choosing photos (licensing)

Do not copy photos from search engines or other sites without permission. Use sources whose licence allows reuse:
Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay, or Wikimedia Commons (check the file's licence; CC BY and CC BY-SA require credit).
Prefer photos of South African community sport where you can find them, and avoid identifiable children.

Guidance for files: landscape, about 1600 x 900 px, JPEG, under 300 KB (the carousel is wide, cards are cropped).
Record every photo below so credit can be shown if the licence needs it.
